Book a taxi in three taps.

RideNow is a college-project prototype: pick a pickup and destination, see an estimated fare, and watch a demo driver move on the map.

Book a taxi Open driver dashboard

Features

Map booking

Choose places on an OpenStreetMap map or use your device location.

Fare estimate

Base fare plus per-kilometre rate. Demonstration values only.

Demo live GPS

A simulated driver moves smoothly to pickup, then destination.

Two dashboards

Customer and driver views stay in sync, even across tabs in one browser.

How it works

  1. Customer picks locations and books.
  2. Driver goes online and accepts.
  3. Driver starts Demo GPS; the customer sees the taxi approach.
  4. Driver marks arrival, starts, and completes the ride.

Simulated GPS is always labelled DEMO GPS. Roles are chosen, not authenticated: a frontend-only prototype cannot provide real accounts.
